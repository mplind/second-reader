# Synthesis

What this vault, taken together, says about the history of the bicycle. This page is
interpretation, not sourced fact, and claims here point back to the pages and sources
that support them.

The modern bicycle is the sum of three changes recorded in [[Bicycle history excerpt]]:
the chain-driven rear wheel of the [[Safety bicycle]] pattern of 1885, two
similar-sized wheels, and the pneumatic tire of 1888. Everything else in the vault,
including [[When did chain drive appear]], hangs off that sequence: a fixed front-wheel
crank tied speed to wheel diameter, and the chain removed the constraint.
