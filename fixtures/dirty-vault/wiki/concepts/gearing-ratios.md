# Gearing Ratios

Why the chain mattered: with a chain between chainring and sprocket, one pedal turn
can drive the rear wheel through more than one revolution, so road speed no longer
depends on wheel diameter ([[Bicycle history excerpt]], second paragraph). This is the
mechanism that let the [[Safety bicycle]] use two small wheels.
