# Pneumatic tires

John Boyd Dunlop's pneumatic tire of 1888 completed the modern bicycle form
([[Bicycle history excerpt]], third paragraph). The trade press of the late 1890s
records pneumatic tires dominating new [[Safety bicycle]] sales, with solid tires
persisting on delivery machines (`raw/inbox/tire-market-note.md`).

Pneumatic tires held ~95% of the new safety bicycle market by the end of the decade.
