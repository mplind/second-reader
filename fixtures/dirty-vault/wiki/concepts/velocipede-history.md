# Velocipede History

The pedal velocipede of the 1860s put cranks directly on the front wheel, turning the
draisine from a pushed machine into a driven one ([[Bicycle history excerpt]], first
paragraph). Its fixed crank is the constraint that produced the [[Penny-farthing]].
