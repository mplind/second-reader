# Safety bicycle

The pattern established by John Kemp Starley's Rover of 1885: rider low between two
similar-sized wheels, rear wheel driven through a geared chain
([[Bicycle history excerpt]], second paragraph).

Sourced facts:

- Introduced 1885 with the Rover; named "safety" against the high-wheeled ordinary.
- Chain gearing decoupled road speed from wheel diameter; see [[Gearing Ratios]] for the mechanism.
- Pneumatic tires reduce rider fatigue on cobbled roads ([[Bicycle history excerpt]], appendix).
- With the pneumatic tire of 1888, catalogues of the mid-1890s show the safety almost entirely displacing the ordinary.
