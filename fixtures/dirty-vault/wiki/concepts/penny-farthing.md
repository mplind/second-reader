# Penny-farthing

The high-wheeled ordinary of the 1870s. With the pedal cranks fixed to the front
wheel, speed was tied to wheel diameter, so makers enlarged the driven wheel until the
rider sat over it ([[Bicycle history excerpt]], first paragraph). The
[[Safety bicycle]] made the design obsolete within a decade.
