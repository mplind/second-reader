# When did chain drive appear

**Question.** When did rear-wheel chain drive appear on bicycles?

**Answer.** On the evidence in this vault: 1885, with John Kemp Starley's Rover, the
first widely copied [[Safety bicycle]] ([[Bicycle history excerpt]], second paragraph).
The same source also uses the coarser phrasing that chain-driven safeties spread
through the late 1880s; the grain difference is tracked in the contradictions ledger.
