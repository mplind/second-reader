# Bicycle history excerpt

Source page for `raw/inbox/bicycle-history-excerpt.md`, a short overview of bicycle
development from the draisine to the safety bicycle.

Key claims, all from this source:

- 1817: Karl von Drais builds the steerable, foot-pushed draisine (first paragraph).
- 1860s: pedal cranks fixed to the front wheel produce the velocipede (first paragraph).
- 1885: Starley's Rover pairs similar-sized wheels with a geared rear chain, the [[Safety bicycle]] pattern (second paragraph).
- 1888: Dunlop's pneumatic tire completes the modern form (third paragraph).
- The appendix notes, with an explicit hedge, that pneumatic tires may reduce rider fatigue on cobbled roads (appendix; never systematically measured).
