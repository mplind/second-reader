# Synthesis

What this vault, taken together, says about the history of the bicycle. This page is
interpretation, not sourced fact, and claims here point back to the pages and sources
that support them.

The modern bicycle is the sum of three changes recorded in [[Bicycle history excerpt]]:
the chain-driven rear wheel of 1885, two similar-sized wheels, and the pneumatic tire
of 1888. The shift away from the [[Penny
Farthing]] followed directly once chain gearing decoupled speed from wheel diameter.
